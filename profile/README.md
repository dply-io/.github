## What is dply?

dply is an infrastructure operations platform for teams that ship. You keep
ownership of your servers; dply gives you the control plane — provisioning,
deploys, runtimes, networking, and observability — without the lock-in of a
black-box PaaS.

- **Bring your own servers** — connect any Linux box, or provision one through
  Hetzner, DigitalOcean, AWS, Linode, and more.
- **Deploy from git** — zero-downtime atomic deploys, deploy hooks, rollbacks,
  and a deploy pipeline you can read.
- **Multi-runtime** — PHP/Laravel, Rails, Node, static sites, containers, and
  serverless functions — not PHP-only.
- **Edge & Cloud** — ship container and edge apps through the managed dply
  surface, or run everything on VMs you control.
- **Batteries included** — TLS automation, managed databases & caches, cron,
  background workers, firewall, monitoring, and snapshots/backups.
- **API & CLI first** — every action is scriptable. Automate from CI or your
  terminal.
